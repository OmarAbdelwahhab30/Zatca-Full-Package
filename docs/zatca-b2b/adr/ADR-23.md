# ADR-23 — Held company sales

* **Status:** Proposed — final once its "Verify" items close
* **Source:** blueprint §34

Options: A auto-issue Simplified to the student; B hold + admin decision. Chosen: **B**. Why: student asked for a company invoice; issuing in the student's name silently violates "never fall back". Verify: D-08 + advisor (time limits for issuing invoices).
